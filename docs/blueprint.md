# **App Name**: DiamondPlan

## Core Features:

- Player Survey: a form where players answer questions about motivations, coaching method preferences, desired outcomes/what they want to work on.
- Player Profile: Summary of the player’s survey responses so player and coach both understand the most appropriate coaching approach.
- AI Profile Summary: Generate a streamlined, easily understandable summary of a player’s profile, acting as a tool for coaches.
- Feedback Submission: Allow the player to provide feedback on the coaching plan’s efficacy and evolving preferences.
- Incorporate statistics: Leverage updated performance statistics (e.g., wOBA, WRC+, K%, BB%, FB%, etc.) and measurements (e.g., exit velocity, spin rate, etc.) as a basis for suggestions on what to work on with the player next and how to work with him on it.
- Roster Access: Provide coaches and organization members access to a roster listing all players with survey results and profiles.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep navy blue (#003087), evoking the classic look of baseball uniforms and conveying trust, stability, and authority. This hue references the tradition and structure of baseball.
- Background color: Very light blue-gray (#F0F4F8), almost white, providing a neutral backdrop that enhances readability and minimizes distractions.
- Accent color: Muted teal (#73A9AD), analogous to navy, providing a refreshing but understated modern feel for highlights and interactive elements.
- Font pairing: 'Belleza' (sans-serif) for headlines and short chunks of text, paired with 'Alegreya' (serif) for body text.
- Clean, card-based layout for player profiles and survey results, ensuring information is well-organized and easy to digest.
- Use a set of consistent icons throughout the app to represent various aspects of baseball player profiles and coaching methods. Opt for line icons that are modern and easily legible, and ideally baseball-focused.
- Subtle transitions and animations for loading data and navigating between sections, providing a smooth user experience.
